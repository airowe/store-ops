## Findings

### 1. `calm` is burned twice — reclaim 5 chars — **fix in next version**

The keyword field spends 4 chars on `calm`, which already appears in the
subtitle (*"Stoic **calm** for atheists"*).

Apple indexes title + subtitle + keyword field **together**. A term in the
subtitle is already indexed; repeating it in the keyword field buys nothing.

```
current  (96/100): mindfulness,calm,stress,sleep,anxiety,breathe,stoicism,focus,journal,affirmation,relax,gratitude
```

Dropping `calm` frees **5 chars** (4 + the comma), leaving 9 total. Candidates
that fit and aren't indexed anywhere today: `grief` (5), `quiet` (5), `atheist`
— note `atheists` is in the subtitle, but the **singular** is not indexed.

**Highest-value, lowest-risk change on this list.**

### 2. Four keywords appear nowhere in your description — **verify, don't assume**

`stress`, `anxiety`, `breathe`, `relax` are in the keyword field but absent
from the description body.

This is **not automatically wrong** — the keyword field exists precisely so you
can target terms the copy doesn't use. Flagging it because it usually means one
of two things:

- the terms are genuinely off-positioning for a Stoic/secular app (in which
  case they're 27 chars that could go to better terms), or
- the description is under-serving searchers who arrive on those terms and find
  no matching language.

`anxiety` and `stress` in particular are high-intent meditation queries. Worth a
deliberate call, not a default.

### 3. No app preview video — **the biggest single lever, and the slowest**

7 screenshots on both 6.7" and 6.5", zero preview videos.

Preview video is consistently one of the largest conversion levers on the
product page — it autoplays in search results, where screenshots do not.

This is real production work, not a metadata edit. It does **not** block the
next release; it's the thing to schedule after.

### 4. Subtitle has 7 unused characters — **optional**

`Stoic calm for atheists` is 23/30. It reads well and I would not pad it just to
fill the field — but if you want a term indexed and can't fit it in keywords,
there's room here.

### 5. Zero ratings — **not an ASO finding**

`userRatingCount: 0`, and the reviews endpoint returned nothing.

Ratings are a ranking input, so this genuinely suppresses discovery. But it's a
lifecycle fact for an app released four days ago, not a listing defect. Nothing
in the metadata can fix it — an in-app review prompt at a good moment can.

**Note:** this is why ShipASO's own audit of Heathen reported *"Too few reviews
to summarize sentiment reliably"* rather than a sentiment score. That is the
measured-or-nothing rule working correctly.
