## Name — B

`Swoop: Chat & Meet IRL` (22/30) — 8 chars unused.

Good brand+keyword structure. But rank data shows `irl` only reaches #119 and
plain `meet people` doesn't place at all, so the name's keywords aren't winning
their terms. **`friends` appears 29 times across competitor listings — the
single most common term in this category — and is absent from both name and
subtitle.**

## Subtitle — B+

`Meet people near you, no login` (**30/30, exactly full**)

The strongest field. Every term distinct from the name, no waste, and it earns
the app's best rank (`no login chat` #64). The "no login" hook is both the real
differentiator and the only thing measurably working in search. Leave it alone
unless keyword research displaces a term with data.

## Keyword field — C (and inert)

`in-real-life, meeting, connecting, new friends, chatting, dating, IRL` — 69/100

Quantified waste:

1. **6 chars** — spaces after commas. Apple splits on the comma; the space is a
   literal character buying nothing.
2. **5 chars** — `IRL` duplicates the app *name*. Name + subtitle + keyword
   field index as one pool; repeating is dead weight.
3. **Stem collisions** — `meeting` vs `Meet` (in name *and* subtitle);
   `in-real-life` vs `IRL` is the same concept twice.

**~37 of 100 characters recoverable.** And per the rank table, none of the
field's unique terms currently place — so this is the clearest lever available.

Absent entirely: the venue/event vocabulary the product actually lives in
(bar, concert, festival, party, event) and the anonymity angle (`no account`,
`anonymous`, `private`) that the subtitle proves converts.

## Description — D

**329 of 4000 characters — 8% used.**

The copy is good: the no-login/ephemeral-photo angle is a genuine
differentiator, stated plainly. Three problems:

1. **~3,670 unused characters.** Doesn't feed iOS keyword ranking directly, but
   drives conversion and is where feature clarity belongs.
2. **The hook is buried.** Only ~3 lines show before "more"; the opener spends
   them on one run-on sentence.
3. **Typo: "an user account"** → "a user account", in the visible portion.

Copy nit: "Yes, it's that simple and awesome" is self-congratulatory in a way
the rest of the copy isn't — the privacy claims earn attention on their own.

## Prioritized fixes

1. **Rewrite the keyword field.** Drop the comma-spaces (6 chars), drop `IRL`
   (5), resolve the `meeting`/`Meet` and `in-real-life`/`IRL` stem collisions.
   Refill with venue/event terms and the anonymity angle. Highest leverage — a
   third of the field is recoverable and none of it is currently ranking.
2. **Get `friends` into the name or subtitle.** The category's dominant term
   (29 uses across competitors), absent from Swoop's two heaviest fields. The
   subtitle is full at 30/30, so this is a trade, not an addition — decide
   deliberately.
3. **Expand the description.** Keep the privacy hook, restructure so the first
   3 lines carry it, add feature clarity. Fix "an user account".
4. **Review volume.** 2 ratings in 6 months. An in-app prompt at a natural
   success moment likely beats metadata tuning.
5. **Resolve the screenshot read** (API key role/scope), then re-score
   dimensions properly.
6. **Decide on iPad.**
